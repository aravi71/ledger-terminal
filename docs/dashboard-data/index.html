How this was tested

  • Daily bars from Yahoo Finance. The signal is computed at each day's close and the trade is filled at the next day's open, so no future prices are used.
  • Long only, one position per market, full 10,000 capital per position, fractional units allowed.
  • Trading costs: slippage of 2 basis points per fill in US markets, 5 in India and 10 in crypto. No brokerage fees, taxes or dividends are included, which slightly understates buy and hold.
  • Default parameters, not tuned to these markets: SMA 50/200, breakout 20-day high in and 10-day low out, RSI 14 in below 30 and out above 55.
  • The markets were chosen knowing they exist today and mostly did well (survivorship bias). Indian indices cannot be bought directly; an index fund or ETF tracks them with small differences.
  • Produced by Ledger Terminal's backtester, the same code its paper-trading bots run.
