How the tests were run

  • Data: Dukascopy spot XAUUSD, US30 and NAS100 candles. 10 years of hourly bars, plus minute bars for the 5- and 15-minute tests (gold 2 years; indices a shorter, preliminary sample).
  • Entries at the next bar's open. Stop-loss checked before take-profit when both fall inside one bar. Buys pay the spread; shorts stop on the ask.
  • Costs: gold $0.30/oz spread plus commission; NAS100 1.0 and US30 2.0 points of spread; estimated overnight swap at the 17:00 New York rollover; 1:100 leverage; minimum size 1 oz (gold) or 0.1 (indices).
  • Past results do not predict future returns. Live trading adds slippage, requotes and spread spikes that a backtest can only approximate.
Full method and results: docs/HANDBOOK.md sections 7b and 7c in this repository. Reproduce with python scripts/research.py --symbol XAUUSD --tf 60.
