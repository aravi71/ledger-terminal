What was researched

252 YouTube videos about gold, US30 and NAS100 strategies were collected from 20 searches. The transcripts of the most-viewed videos with real rules were read, and about 40 Instagram reels were checked. Every strategy with clear rules was coded exactly and backtested on gold (10 years hourly, 2 years of 1- and 5-minute data), NAS100 and US30 (10 years hourly, 2 years of minute data), with real spreads, commission, overnight fees and broker minimum sizes. Settings were chosen on the first 70% of each history and judged on the last 30%.

About 80–90% win rates and 30–40% a month

Win rates of 70–92% are real for strategies with a small target and a wide stop (NAS100's RSI(2) setup won 91% of over 1,000 trades in 10 years) — and they ended roughly break-even after costs. No strategy, market or risk level reached 30–40% a month in the tests; getting close required 20%+ risk per trade, where most simulated accounts halved within a year. Use the Goal check on the Strategies tab to see the numbers for any strategy.

Connecting XM later

This page is static (GitHub Pages), so it only paper-trades in your browser. Real or demo orders at XM need a small server, because XM trades through MetaTrader 4/5 and your login must never be placed in a public web page or repository.

  1. Open an XM demo account (MT5) first. Do not use a live account until a strategy has months of good paper and demo results.
  2. Run Ledger Terminal on a Windows PC or Windows VPS with the MetaTrader 5 terminal logged in to that demo account.
  3. Add a broker adapter using MetaQuotes' official MetaTrader5 Python package (it talks to the local terminal: prices, orders with stop-loss/take-profit, positions).
  4. Keep the same safety rules as the paper bots: per-trade risk, daily loss limit, kill switch, and the 1% default.

XM symbols are usually GOLD, US30Cash and US100Cash; contract sizes differ from these paper settings, so position sizes must be recalculated from XM's own contract specification.
